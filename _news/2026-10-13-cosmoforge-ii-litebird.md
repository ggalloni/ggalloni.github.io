Two new preprints. **CosmoForge II** extends the CosmoForge QML estimator to filtered maps,
treating arbitrary linear filters exactly ([arXiv:2610.18012](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18012)).
For **LiteBIRD**, I led the validation of harmonic-space likelihood methods for the mission's
$$B$$-mode analysis ([arXiv:2610.18036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18036)).
